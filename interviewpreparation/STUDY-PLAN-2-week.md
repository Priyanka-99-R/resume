# Priyanka — Interview Prep Study Plan (2 Weeks)

**Source:** Priyanka-Interview-Preparation-Pack.pdf (144 pages, sections 00–14)
**Target:** Berribot — Senior Full Stack (Java 17 · Spring Boot · Angular 16 · MongoDB · MySQL · Microservices · AWS S3)
**Start:** Wed 1 Jul 2026 → **Interview-ready:** Tue 14 Jul 2026
**Daily budget:** ~2–3 hrs weekdays, ~3–4 hrs weekends. Adjust to your schedule.

### Guiding strategy
- **Front-load your gaps** (MongoDB, Java 17) so you have time to revisit them, not cram them last.
- **Lead with strength** (Angular, Spring Boot, Projects) — most interview time lands here.
- **Self-intro + Projects get touched early AND revised throughout** — they're asked in every round.
- **Say answers out loud. Type the code yourself.** Passive reading ≠ prepared.
